Quaint coffee roastery / cafe near the entrance to Sequoia National Park. They really lean into the Kaweah (raven) theme for a pretty fun experience, various ravens placed in and around the shop. Manual pressed espresso, and fresh ground beans for all coffee based drinks. There is a small stage type corner for what seems like live music.
Pretty amazing experience overall.
